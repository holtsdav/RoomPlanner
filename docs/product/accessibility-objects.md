# Accessibility planning objects

The Accessibility library provides plan-view objects and clear-space overlays. They help lay out a room; they do not certify a design. Preset dimensions use rounded metric planning sizes or U.S. Access Board reference dimensions. Check local requirements and actual products before building.

| Object                                  | Preset basis                                                                      | What the footprint means                                                                                                                  |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Wheelchair ramp                         | 75, 150, or 250 mm sample rises; 1:12, 1:16, and 1:20 grades                      | Width × **sloped run only**. The displayed angle is `atan(rise / run)`. The run does not include landings, handrails, or edge protection. |
| Ramp landing                            | 1525 × 1525 mm                                                                    | Clear level planning area.                                                                                                                |
| Wheelchair turning space                | 1525 mm circle or T-turn envelope                                                 | Keep-clear maneuvering area. The T symbol shows a 915 mm wide stem within the envelope.                                                   |
| Clear floor space                       | 760 × 1220 mm, rotated for side approach                                          | Keep-clear approach area for a fixture, worktop, or appliance.                                                                            |
| Kitchen aisle clearance                 | 1015 mm pass-through or 1525 mm U-shaped aisle, each with editable 2000 mm length | Keep-clear aisle strip. Its sample length is not a standard minimum.                                                                      |
| Knee-space worktop and kitchen sink     | Representative 900/1200 mm worktops and an 800 mm sink                            | Physical top-view counter footprint. Height and knee/toe dimensions are outside this 2D plan.                                             |
| Wall handrail, grab bar, pull-out shelf | Representative sizes                                                              | Physical footprints. Mounting height, structure, extension, and reach are outside this 2D plan.                                           |

The [U.S. Access Board ramp guide](https://www.access-board.gov/ada/guides/chapter-4-ramps-and-curb-ramps/) describes a 1:12 maximum running slope in the ADA Standards, at least 36 inches (915 mm) clear width, and level landings at each end. Its guide also covers handrails and edge protection. The [turning and clear-floor guide](https://www.access-board.gov/ada/guides/chapter-3-clear-floor-or-ground-space-and-turning-space/) describes a 60 inch (1525 mm) turning circle or T-turn and 30 × 48 inch (760 × 1220 mm) clear floor space. [Kitchen provisions](https://www.access-board.gov/ada/chapter/ch08/) give 40 inch (1015 mm) pass-through and 60 inch (1525 mm) U-shaped aisle widths, as well as work-surface and approach requirements. These references explain the choices; the library does not apply a jurisdiction's full rules.
